================================================================================
PROJECT DESCRIPTION: SMOKEFREE
Tagline: Breathe Fresh, Live Free
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SmokeFree is a bilingual (English & Tamil), dual-portal mobile health application 
developed with Flutter. It connects individuals recovering from nicotine dependence 
with their doctors and pulmonologists. The app pairs real-time, AI-powered cognitive 
and physiological craving management for patients with a clinical telemetry and risk 
triaging dashboard for healthcare providers.


2. PURPOSE & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The Problem:
  - Acute tobacco cravings typically peak and dissipate within a 3 to 5 minute 
    window. During these intense situational triggers (stress, meals, social pressure), 
    individuals rarely have access to immediate cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) 
    or clinical support.
  - Doctors and healthcare providers only evaluate patients weeks apart in clinics, 
    leaving them blind to day-to-day craving spikes, emotional triggers, and minor 
    slips until full relapse has already occurred.

* The Purpose:
  - SmokeFree equips patients with an on-demand "Craving SOS Shield" right when 
    impulses strike, while granting clinicians continuous visibility into patient 
    recovery adherence, risk ratings, and slip telemetry to prevent relapse.


3. TARGET AUDIENCE (WHO IT SERVES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Patients / Quitters:
   Individuals seeking to quit smoking or tobacco use who require real-time craving 
   rescue tools, recovery metrics, motivational feedback, and non-judgmental guidance 
   in either English or Tamil.

2. Healthcare Providers / Doctors / Pulmonologists:
   Clinicians supervising smoking cessation cohorts who need real-time patient 
   telemetry, risk stratification, slip alerts, and customizable digital 
   intervention strategies.

3. Clinical Administrators & Support Staff:
   Clinic personnel managing patient onboarding, doctor rosters, and baseline 
   intake data.


4. MAIN GOALS & EXPECTED OUTCOMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Break Craving Impulse Loops: 
  Provide instant grounding using the evidence-based "4Ds" protocol (Deep Breathe, 
  Delay, Drink Water, Distract).

* Personalized Cognitive Support: 
  Deliver adaptive Empathy (guilt-free cognitive reframing) and Aversion (medical 
  and financial consequences) interventions powered by an AI Behavioral Engine.

* Proactive Clinical Triage: 
  Automatically identify high-risk patients (craving intensity >= 7/10 or reported 
  slips) on the doctor dashboard for early intervention.

* Long-Term Motivation & Habit Rewiring: 
  Track and celebrate smoke-free days, money saved (in INR), avoided cigarettes, 
  and biological health milestones, supported by a throttled gamification system.


5. WHAT IS IN SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Patient Portal:
  - Live Recovery Dashboard: 
    Live counters for smoke-free duration, money saved, avoided cigarettes, and 
    validated health recovery milestones (carbon monoxide clearance, lung repair).
  - Craving SOS Shield:
    * 4-7-8 Breathing Pacer: Concentric multi-layer animated orb with haptic cues 
      for vagus nerve activation.
    * 3-Minute Delay Challenge: Interactive timer featuring dynamic reflections 
      synthesized from live personal stats.
    * Hydration Tracker: Interactive water intake logging addressing oral fixation.
    * Cognitive Distraction Suite: 60-second focus puzzles and 5-4-3-2-1 sensory 
      grounding techniques.
  - AI Behavioral Intelligence: 
    Dual-mode Empathy and Aversion recommendation cards using the Google Gemini API 
    with a 100% offline rule-based heuristic clinical fallback.
  - Daily & Bi-Weekly Assessments: 
    Digital questionnaires monitoring mood, craving frequency, and withdrawal signs.
  - Native Bilingual Localization: 
    Seamless UI adaptation across English and Tamil without overflow issues.

B. Doctor Portal:
  - Patient Cohort Dashboard: 
    Patient roster sorting, risk indicators, craving urge scales, and medical 
    profile inspection.
  - AI Cravings & Coping Strategy Center: 
    Peak craving surge telemetry, coping pillar efficacy metrics, and automated 
    dispatch rules.
  - Clinical Prompt & Questionnaire Hub: 
    Customizable morning/evening check-in questions and behavioral prompts.

C. Administration & Platform Security:
  - Role-based authentication (Patient, Doctor, Administrator).
  - Encrypted local session storage using secure hardware-backed keystore/keychain.


6. KEY MILESTONES & TIMELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Milestone 1 — Core Foundation & Tracking [Completed]:
  User authentication, smoke-free timers, money saved algorithms, biological 
  milestones, and basic questionnaire flow.

* Milestone 2 — Dual-Portal & Doctor Dashboard [Completed]:
  Doctor cohort interface, patient risk triaging, detail inspection, and responsive 
  mobile layouts.

* Milestone 3 — AI Behavioral Engine & Bilingual SOS Shield [Completed]:
  Canonical trigger options, personalized dynamic reflections, 4Ds coping suite, 
  Gemini API + offline heuristic fallback, and complete English/Tamil localization.

* Milestone 4 — Cloud Telemetry & Push Notification Integration [Next Phase]:
  Cloud database sync, automated push notifications for daily check-ins, and 
  doctor-to-patient alerts.

* Milestone 5 — Wearables & Clinical Validation [Future Phase]:
  Smartwatch heart-rate variability (HRV) integration for early craving detection, 
  and pilot clinical trials in hospital cessation programs.


7. RESOURCES, DEPENDENCIES & CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Frontend & Framework: 
  Flutter (Dart 3.x), Material 3, Google Fonts (Outfit & Inter), FL Chart, 
  Flutter Animate.

* State Management & Architecture: 
  Provider pattern with separation of concerns (screens, providers, services, 
  models, core).

* Artificial Intelligence: 
  Google Gemini Cloud API paired with an offline heuristic clinical decision engine.

* Backend & Storage: 
  REST APIs (configurable in AppConfig), shared_preferences, flutter_secure_storage.

* Key Constraints:
  - 100% Offline Capability: Craving rescue features must function completely 
    without internet access.
  - Cross-Language Layouts: Tamil font metrics and character lengths require flexible, 
    wrapping, and scroll-safe components to prevent layout overflows.
  - Anti-Abuse Gamification: Client-side cooldown timers (e.g., 15-minute throttle 
    on point claims) prevent exploitation.
